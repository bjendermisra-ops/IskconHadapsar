Our Mission

Our temple is dedicated to spreading spiritual peace, Harinam Sankirtan, the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita and Srimad Bhagavatam, and Vedic education in society.

We invite everyone to experience the pure bliss of devotion, beautiful Darshan, and Karma-free Prasadam.
